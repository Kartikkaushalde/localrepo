# Analyst Findings

Based on the included synthetic sample:

1. Technology is the strongest revenue category because laptops, tablets and monitors have higher transaction values.
2. Revenue should not be evaluated alone; profit and margin are included to identify low-quality discounted sales.
3. Regional comparison helps identify where sales volume and profitability are concentrated.
4. Customer-level revenue highlights high-value customers who can be targeted for retention campaigns.
5. Monthly aggregation provides a simple framework for monitoring growth and identifying changes in demand.
6. Discount analysis can be used to balance conversion with profitability.

## Recommended Business Actions

- Prioritize high-revenue technology products while monitoring margin.
- Create retention offers for high-value repeat customers.
- Review high-discount transactions for margin leakage.
- Track monthly revenue and profit together rather than revenue alone.
